3.1

Click a box

Make the box react: one click fills it, the next click empties it again.

Three things are needed: something that remembers if the box is filled, a click handler, and a look that follows what is remembered.

The big difference is cross-comment 4. In Quasar and Vue the click is handled in the browser. In NiceGUI it travels to your Python on the server, and the change travels back.

ref(false) remembers, @click flips it, and :style adds the dark background while it is true.

NiceGUI nicegui_app.py · Python · 12 lines

No variable: the box's own classes remember. .on('click', …) runs Python, which toggles the filled class.

Quasar

A click's round trip

In Quasar and Vue, a click never leaves the browser. In NiceGUI it makes a round trip to your Python and back. It is quick enough that you do not notice, and it means your click handler can use anything Python can: files, a database, other libraries.

Try it

Run the NiceGUI column, then stop the server and click the box. The box does not change, and NiceGUI reports that the connection is lost: the click needs Python. The Quasar and Vue columns keep working, because they need only the browser.
